How Porch works

Agents never reach a device platform. They ask Porch; Porch checks who is asking, the home's rules and who is in the room, then drives the device and reads it back.

Porch's rules

Checked on every request, in this order.

  1. Who is asking. An unknown key is refused. An energy agent reads energy and controls nothing.
  2. What it may control. Each agent has its own rights: lights, appliances; locks and water only with the owner.
  3. Who is in the room. If someone else is in the device's room and the agent's own person is not, the person in the room decides, with their PIN.
  4. Family protection. A real device moves only if the owner always allows it, or opened a window for it.
  5. Read back. Porch confirms each change by reading the device itself.

Who is where
